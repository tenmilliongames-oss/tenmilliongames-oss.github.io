Demon Summoner Idle — Privacy Policy (iPhone)

Effective 2026-11-14 (from the first iPhone version) · Developer: 2eex Games · Contact: demonhero@2eexgames.com

This policy applies to Demon Summoner Idle downloaded from the App Store (a free app with ads). The Google Play app has its own policy.

1. What we process and why

  • Account: a user ID the game creates (guest accounts included) and a support account number. If you start or link with Apple or Google, the email address of that account. If you choose "Hide My Email" when signing in with Apple, we receive only the relay address Apple creates. — To identify your account, keep your progress across phones and reinstalls, and answer support requests.
  • Game progress: each character's save (level, gear, currencies, progress), save time and a device label. — To store and restore your progress on our server and to prevent cheating.
  • Profile and rankings: the nickname you choose, your country, class, level, worn gear and hero look (costume), ranking record (damage in one minute) and legend registrations. Your country is set automatically from your App Store country or your phone's region; you can change or hide it. — For the rankings and the legend codex.
  • Payment records: transaction ID, product, quantity, purchase time, whether it was a test purchase, and refund time and reason code. — To verify purchases, prevent double grants and handle refunds.
  • Usage statistics: in-game actions (screens opened, progress, currency flow, purchases, ad results, frame times), a random per-install identifier, device model, OS version and language, and an approximate region (country/city) estimated from your IP address. — To improve and balance the game and find errors.
  • Advertising: see section 7.

Card numbers and other payment details are handled by Apple and never reach us. The game does not access location permissions, contacts or photos. Game notifications are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to our server for them.

2. What other players see

Your nickname, country (unless hidden), class, level, worn gear and hero look, and ranking record appear in the rankings. The rankings are shared with players of the Google Play app. If you choose no nickname, an automatic name is used.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Account, saves, profile and ranking records: until you delete your account; deletion is immediate.
  • Saves not chosen in a save conflict and saves of deleted characters: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Payment records: kept for 5 years as Korean e-commerce law requires, then deleted automatically. They remain for that period even after the account is deleted.
  • Usage statistics: event-level data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted; only aggregate statistics that identify no one remain.
  • Advertising data: not kept by us; handled under Google's retention policies.
  • Published leaderboards: you drop off at the next daily rebuild.
  • The "first finder" line in the legend codex keeps only a nickname no longer linked to any account.
  • The save on your phone: remove the app, or use Settings → Reset.

4. Service providers and international transfer

We use Google Firebase for our server and statistics. Google processes this data on our behalf and does not sell it or hand it over for advertising. (Advertising is described separately in section 7.)

  • Recipient: Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) · https://support.google.com/policies
  • Where: saves, profiles, rankings and payment records are stored in Seoul, Republic of Korea. Sign-in (Firebase Authentication) and usage statistics (Google Analytics) are processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers.
  • What: the account and usage data of section 1, as needed for sign-in and statistics
  • When and how: when you run the game, save, sync or sign in, encrypted in transit
  • How long: as in section 3
  • Opting out: you can delete your account. Server saves and rankings then stop, and server progress cannot be recovered.

Sign in with Apple and App Store payments are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). To verify purchases and handle refunds, the game looks up the transaction on Apple's servers by its transaction ID.

Apart from the advertising in section 7, we share personal data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not process the data of children under 14 without a legal guardian's consent; if we learn of it, we delete that account's data without delay. Guardians can contact us to access or delete it.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data at any time (including under the GDPR or CCPA where they apply).

  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account in the game, or the account deletion page (https://tenmilliongames-oss.github.io/demonhero/delete-account.html). If you signed in with Apple, deleting the account also disconnects Sign in with Apple.
  • Delete one character: Settings → Reset in the game, choosing to remove it from the server too
  • Change your nickname or country, hide your country, turn off notifications: in the game
  • Stop ad tracking: see section 7
  • Anything else: demonhero@2eexgames.com — we answer within 7 days.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob) — provision to a third party and international transfer

The game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (such as double idle rewards, revive and 2× speed) and an interstitial ad that may show when you leave a battle for the base (at most once every 3 minutes). There are no banner ads. AdMob collects the following and sends it to Google to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud; Google also uses it to run its own advertising services.

  • Recipient: Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) · https://support.google.com/policies
  • Data: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), device information such as the identifier for vendors (IDFV), IP address (may be used to estimate approximate location), in-app activity such as launches, taps and ad views, and diagnostics such as launch time and errors
  • Purpose: ad delivery (including personalized ads), ad measurement, fraud prevention
  • Where: the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers
  • When and how: when the game starts its ads feature and when you watch an ad, encrypted in transit. The ads feature starts with the game, so some data is sent even if you never watch an ad.
  • How long: under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's retention policies
  • Opting out: when the game first asks whether to allow it to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites, choose "Ask App Not to Track" and the advertising identifier is not used. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone. The game works the same without tracking, and ads are shown without the advertising identifier. You can play without watching ads; they only give extra rewards or conveniences.

Ads in the game are limited to content rated Teen (T) or lower.

8. Ad consent

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before ads and asks whether you agree to personalized ads and to storing and accessing information on your device. In these regions you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → "Ad privacy choices". The consent form comes first; the tracking question follows it.

9. Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS), server access limited to the developer, and database rules that stop players reading each other's data.

10. Changes

We announce changes on this page and in the in-game news.
