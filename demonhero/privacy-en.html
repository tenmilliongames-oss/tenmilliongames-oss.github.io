Demon Summoner Idle — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-13 (from version 1.1.0) · Developer: 2eex Games · Contact: demonhero@2eexgames.com

Version 1.1.0 adds server saves, Google sign-in, rankings and in-game purchases, so the game now processes more data. This page says what we process, why, and how to delete it.

1. What we process and why

  1. Account: a user ID the game creates (guest accounts included) and a support account number. If you start or link with Google, the email address of that Google account. — To identify your account, keep your progress across phones and reinstalls, and answer support requests.
  2. Game progress: each character's save (level, gear, currencies, progress), save time and a device label. — To store and restore your progress on our server and to prevent cheating.
  3. Profile and rankings: the nickname you choose, your country, class, level, worn gear and hero look (costume), ranking record (damage in one minute) and legend registrations. Your country is set automatically from your Google Play billing country or your phone's region; you can change or hide it. — For the rankings and the legend codex.
  4. Payment records: order number, product, quantity, purchase time, whether it was a test purchase, and refund time and reason code. The purchase token is used only to verify the purchase and is not stored. — To verify purchases, prevent double grants and handle refunds.
  5. Notifications: a push token (FCM), your notification choice and time zone. — To send event notifications to players who agreed (never between 21:00 and 08:00 local time).
  6. Usage statistics: in-game actions (screens opened, progress, currency flow, frame times), a random per-install identifier, device model, OS version and language, and an approximate region (country/city) estimated from your IP address. — To improve and balance the game and find errors.

We do not collect the advertising ID. Card numbers and other payment details are handled by Google Play and never reach us. The game does not access location permissions, contacts or photos.

2. What other players see

Your nickname, country (unless hidden), class, level, worn gear and hero look, and ranking record appear in the rankings. If you choose no nickname, an automatic name is used.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Account, saves, profile and ranking records: until you delete your account; deletion is immediate.
  • Saves not chosen in a save conflict and saves of deleted characters: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Payment records: kept for 5 years as Korean e-commerce law requires, then deleted automatically. They remain for that period even after the account is deleted.
  • Usage statistics: event-level data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted; only aggregate statistics that identify no one remain.
  • Published leaderboards: you drop off at the next daily rebuild.
  • The "first finder" line in the legend codex keeps only a nickname no longer linked to any account.
  • The save on your phone: remove the app, or use Settings → Reset.

4. Service providers and international transfer

We use Google Firebase for our server and statistics. Google processes data on our behalf and does not sell it or hand it over for advertising.

  • Recipient: Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) · https://support.google.com/policies
  • Where: saves, profiles, rankings and payment records are stored in Seoul, Republic of Korea. Sign-in (Firebase Authentication), notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and usage statistics (Google Analytics) are processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers.
  • What: the account, notification and usage data of section 1, as needed for sign-in, notifications and statistics
  • When and how: when you run the game, save, sync or sign in, encrypted in transit
  • How long: as in section 3
  • Opting out: you can delete your account. Server saves and rankings then stop, and server progress cannot be recovered.

Google's handling follows the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We share personal data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not process the data of children under 14 without a legal guardian's consent; if we learn of it, we delete that account's data without delay. Guardians can contact us to access or delete it.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data at any time (including under the GDPR or CCPA where they apply).

  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account in the game, or the account deletion page
  • Delete one character: Settings → Reset in the game, choosing to remove it from the server too
  • Change your nickname or country, hide your country, turn off notifications: in the game
  • Anything else: demonhero@2eexgames.com — we answer within 7 days.

7. Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS), server access limited to the developer, database rules that stop players reading each other's data, and purchase tokens never stored.

8. Changes

We announce changes on this page and in the in-game news. The previous policy (effective 2026-10-12, versions 1.0.1–1.0.x) covered anonymous usage statistics only, with no accounts or server.
