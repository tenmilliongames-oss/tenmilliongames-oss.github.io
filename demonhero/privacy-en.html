Demon Summoner Idle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-10 · Developer: 2eex Games · Contact: demonhero@2eexgames.com

This game collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no in-game purchases, and it sends no progress data or device information anywhere.

1. Data the game handles

The game has no accounts. Your progress (save data) is stored only on your device. We run no servers and do not receive or keep your game data. The game contains no advertising SDK and no analytics.

2. Purchase

The game is sold as a paid app on Google Play, and Google Play processes the purchase. We never receive payment details. There are no in-game purchases.

3. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

4. Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data. The save on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset.

5. Contact

demonhero@2eexgames.com

6. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the game under Settings → Credits.
