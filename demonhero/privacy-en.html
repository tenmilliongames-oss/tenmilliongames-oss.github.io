Demon Summoner Idle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-12 (from version 1.0.1) · Developer: 2eex Games · Contact: demonhero@2eexgames.com

This game does not ask for your name, contact details or an account. To improve the game, it collects anonymous usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics. There are no ads and no in-game purchases.

1. Game progress

The game has no accounts. Your progress (save data) is stored only on your device; we never receive or keep it.

2. Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To learn how many people play and where they get stuck, so we can tune the difficulty, the game collects:

  • an app instance ID, generated at random for each installation
  • game progress events: class chosen, level ups, first stage clears, first boss kills, stage retreats, rebirths, app opens and session length
  • basic device information such as device model, OS version and language
  • approximate country/region derived from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored)

We do not collect the advertising ID, the Android ID, or your name, email or phone number. The data is used only for statistics; it is not used for advertising and is never sold.

3. Processor and international transfer

Google LLC (United States) operates Firebase Analytics for us. The data above is sent encrypted (TLS) while you use the app. Event-level records are kept for up to 14 months and then deleted; only aggregate statistics that identify no one remain. Google processes the data under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

4. Purchase

The game is sold as a paid app on Google Play, and Google Play processes the purchase. We never receive payment details. There are no in-game purchases.

5. Your choices

If you do not want usage statistics collected, or want collected records deleted, contact us by email. Reinstalling the app creates a new app instance ID that is not linked to earlier records.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the game under Settings → Credits. The previous policy (effective 2026-10-10, version 1.0.0) covered a version that collected no data.
