Affixes

How affixes are picked: uniform among the candidates of the chosen kind (prefix or suffix) that are open for this slot and item level, meet the base item's conditions, and do not overlap an affix already on the item. Up to 2 affixes from the same group (elemental damage increase, resistance) can appear.

Value: uniform between the tier range's minimum and maximum

Skill +1 base items (Wand): when a prefix is picked, first a 20% chance of 'Masterful'

The 'Masterful' affix starts at T3, so the share of lower tiers in the window is removed.

Only the 5 highest tiers open for that affix can roll, weighted 40 : 30 : 18 : 9 : 3 from lowest to highest. On Magic items, the highest open tier's weight is ×2.

Reroll · Imprint

Full reroll: a new affix list for the item's rarity (affix rules above)

Value reroll: affixes and tiers stay; only values are rerolled, uniform within the tier range

Locked reroll: locked affixes stay; the rest are rolled by the rules above to a total of 3–5 (uniform)

Rarity upgrade (Magic to Rare): adds 1–3 affixes, uniform (up to 5)

Imprint: the chosen affix is added at the highest open tier for the item level; only its value is uniform

Add socket · Tier upgrade · Gem fusion · Gem removal: nothing random

Your first full reroll is free. If the item being rerolled is one of Weapon·Off-hand·Gloves·Ring·Amulet, it always gets 'Sharp' T1 at the top value, and the other affixes follow the affix rules above. Other slots work like a normal reroll.

Enhancement

Each enhancement level multiplies weapon damage by (1 + 0.06 × level) (+15 = ×1.90); armour multiplies life by (1 + the four pieces' levels ÷ 120) (all four at +15 = ×1.50); the top is +15. From +6 a failure drops one level, but +5 · +10 are floors it never falls below, and an item is never destroyed. A guard keeps the level on a failure.

A transfer is certain, with no chance involved: the level moves to a +0 item of the same slot and the same or a higher item level, and the old item goes back to +0. Stones = 25% of the stones the level costs with guards × min(1, item levels gained ÷ 10), rounded.

Guard: one is used only when a failure would drop the level, and it keeps the level (the smith puts guards in play from +7). It does not change the success chance and is not used on a failure that would not drop.

Enhancement stones drop from bosses, up to 1 / 2 / 4 a day (surface / stairs floors 1–50 / floor 51 on, counted across all characters), plus 1 from the five-goal daily reward inside the same cap.

Legend enhancement

Legendary gear uses its own table. Stones cost 3× the normal step, and guards work only on attempts up to +10. A failure on a floor (+5 · +10 · +12) keeps the level, so once at +12 a legend never drops below it. The top is +15, and gear is never destroyed.

Pity: every failed attempt on a legend from +10 adds 1 to that item's gauge (max 10). At 10, the next attempt succeeds 100% and the gauge goes back to 0. A success at the table's chance leaves the gauge as it is.

Transfer puts at most +10 on a legend (a certain operation, no chance involved). A legend's level cannot be moved to other gear.

Legend Reforge Stone

1 Legend Reforge Stone rerolls the values of the four lines of one legendary item you hold. Each legend's lines are fixed and do not change; the new values replace the old ones. This cannot be undone.

The four lines are rolled independently. Each line's value is the band minimum + (maximum − minimum) × r, with r drawn uniformly between 0 and 1. The band is the highest one the legendary item's level unlocks.

Values are rounded to whole numbers, so the two end values of a band (minimum and maximum) each have half the chance of a value between them. With a gap of k between minimum and maximum, each end value has 1/(2k) and each value between has 1/k; when minimum and maximum are equal, that value is 100%.

A legendary item's level rises with its wearer's deepest progress in this life. When a new band opens, the rolled position r is kept and only the value moves into the new band.

The first time you get a legendary item you receive 1 free reforge stone each for the weapon and the ring. A reroll uses free stones first.

Enhancement bonus lines

An item gains one bonus line each time it reaches +10 · +15 (legendary items excluded). Which line it gets is fixed for each item from the start and cannot be drawn again, and a failed enhancement never removes it. A level moved by a transfer brings the lines fixed for the receiving item.

The line is drawn with equal odds from the candidates in the table below. A line the item already has is left out (the rest share equal odds), and the +15 line is drawn from the candidates left after the +10 line. The table shows the odds when nothing is left out.

The value involves no chance: it is the average of the same line as a normal roll at the item's level, times the share below.

Seventh summon gift

When the seventh summon slot opens at Lv77, you receive one of the summons below as a gift. The result is fixed per character and cannot be drawn again; summons you already brought in are left out (the rest share equal odds). If you have them all, you receive 100 Enhance Stones instead.

Other summons are chosen and brought in at a fixed price of 100 Enhance Stones. There is no random purchase.

Summon enhancement never fails: HP and damage +4% per step, up to +10, 484 Enhance Stones to reach +10.
