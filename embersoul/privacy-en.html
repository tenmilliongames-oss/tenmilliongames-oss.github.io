Demon Summoner Idle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-06 Developer: 2eex Games · Contact: demonhero@2eexgames.com

1. Data the game handles itself

The game has no accounts. Your progress (save data) is stored only on your device. We run no servers and do not receive or keep your game data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show rewarded video ads the game uses the Google AdMob SDK. AdMob collects the following and sends it to Google to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address (may be used to estimate approximate location)
  • in-app interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • diagnostics such as launch time and crash rate

This data is encrypted in transit and handled under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy or Google → Ads). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown before ads.

3. Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive card numbers or other payment details; the game only records on your device which items were granted.

4. International transfer

The data in section 2 is transferred to Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) and processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates, for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, under Google's retention policies. You can opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings; the game keeps working and shows non-personalised ads.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep no personal data. Delete the app, or use Settings → Reset in the game, to remove your save.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data. As we hold none, requests about advertising data go through your phone's settings or Google. For anything else, contact demonhero@2eexgames.com.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the in-game credits.
